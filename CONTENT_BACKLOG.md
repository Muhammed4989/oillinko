# Oillinko blog content backlog

Topic queue for the automated daily content pipeline (see `.claude`-scheduled
task "oillinko-daily-content" — or ask Claude "run the daily content task").
Two unchecked topics are picked from the TOP of this list on each run, in
order, then checked off `[x]` once the article is written and committed.

**Scope is the whole oil & gas industry, not just equipment we sell.**
Anything genuinely related to oil and gas trading, production, extraction,
transportation, testing/laboratories or industry technology belongs here —
not only topics that map to an Oillinko equipment category or a direct RFQ
angle. Keep the equipment/procurement articles coming too, but don't force
every article back to "browse our category" or "send us your RFQ" if it
doesn't fit naturally (e.g. a market-pricing or drilling-technique article
usually shouldn't end with an equipment pitch).

When fewer than 10 topics remain unchecked, the next run should generate
~20 more (same broad scope, same non-fabrication and citation rules as
CONTENT_PIPELINE.md) and append them here before continuing.
