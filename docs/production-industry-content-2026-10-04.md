## Research scope

API's public announcement identifies API 6A as Wellhead and Tree Equipment:
https://www.api.org/products-and-services/standards/important-standards-announcements/spec-6a
It is a historical 21st-edition announcement, not proof of the latest edition
or access to normative text. The guide requires the project's applicable edition
and does not derive universal acceptance classes or operating limits.

The primary-source artificial-lift overview reviewed for selection inputs is
recorded in the private batch research record. It supports the dependence on
well geometry, fluids, production conditions and surface infrastructure. No
manufacturer identity, product model, performance promise or assigned supplier
appears in the industry content.

IOGP JIP33 describes coordinated technical, quality, information and procurement
data requirements, supplementing governing standards:
https://jip33.iogp.org/about/faq/
The guide uses this procurement distinction without claiming that any unspecified
package is compliant. The remaining package and comparison questions are original
purchasing analysis. No field procedure, well design or production forecast is given.
