# Gas and retirement sector guides — 3 October 2026

These are independent procurement guides on two existing sector pages, not additional blog articles or new categories. Primary terms are `gas treatment package enquiry` and `decommissioning procurement scope`. Supporting terms include feed gas analysis, flow reference conditions, dehydration and sweetening scope, asset inventory, materials recovery and restoration acceptance. No search volume or ranking is claimed. The current search results emphasize process-chain explanations and retirement programmes; the guides answer the buyer's distinct question of which information and work boundaries to include in an enquiry.

The gas guide connects composition, reference conditions, outlet targets and compressor auxiliaries. It complements the individual equipment pages and the existing power/refinery guides without repeating their complete-plant utility or turnaround subjects. The retirement guide focuses on asset disposition, waste characterization and close-out, extending the existing offshore guide's equipment enquiry context. Each has more than 700 independent paragraph words, linked through central catalogue IDs and the existing guide component.

- [EIA: Delivery and storage of natural gas](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/natural-gas/delivery-and-storage.php): confirms multiple possible treatment stages depending on feed, with dehydration and contaminant removal serving different purposes. Only that broad process distinction is paraphrased; it is not a process selection rule or operating instruction.
- [OPRED: offshore installations and pipeline decommissioning](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/oil-and-gas-decommissioning-of-offshore-installations-and-pipelines), updated 25 September 2026: provides the UK-specific programme/inventory context, draft and approved programmes and close-out material. The guide labels this as one jurisdictional example and does not apply UK legislation to other projects.
- [HSE: installation integrity guidance L85](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/l85.htm): identifies installation integrity and decommissioning context. It does not supply a removal procedure or prove any contractor's qualification.
