# Maintenance-service scope review

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Existing tank-cleaning, heat-exchanger maintenance, hydrostatic-testing and equipment-repair pages have individual enquiry paragraphs and three original comparison/handover sections each. Existing topic IDs, routes, old information-N anchors and Oillinko manual enquiry routing remain unchanged.

Primary editorial basis:

- [HSE confined-space introduction](https://www.hse.gov.uk/confinedspace/introduction.htm): tank work needs competent risk assessment and planning. The copy distinguishes a service enquiry and cleaning completion from entry authorization or operational release. UK guidance is background, not a claim that UK legislation governs every project.
- [HSE GS4 pressure-testing guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/gs4.htm): pressure testing involves stored energy and requires appropriate planning and controls. The copy requests an approved test basis, boundaries and reporting; it supplies no test pressure, procedure, isolation arrangement or operating instruction.
- [TEMA official standards overview](https://tema.org/standards/) and [support information](https://support.tema.org/): exchanger specification and documented configuration matter. The current public information identifies the 2026 edition and a transition period; the page copy does not select a mandatory edition or reproduce subscription-only technical text.
- [API RP 687 publication announcement](https://www.api.org/products-and-services/standards/important-standards-announcements/rp687-tradepress): inspection, component repair and repair-shop selection have equipment-specific scopes. The copy does not impose special-purpose rotating-equipment guidance on unrelated assets or claim a shop's qualification.

Other text is original procurement planning. It defines assessment and approval stages, assumptions, acceptance evidence and handover. No residue classification, diagnosis, technical guarantee, assigned specialist, supplier identity, price or hazardous work method is invented. This is supplementary section editing, with no extra blog article or Google post.
