# Drilling industry procurement guide — 4 October 2026

Add an independent original guide to the existing `/industries/drilling` page.
It previously had the sector introduction and filtered catalogue but no sector
guide. The guide coordinates seven buying questions: work packages, rig
interfaces, tubulars, circulation duty, cementing/well-control boundaries,
inspection evidence, and commercial/delivery comparison. Six related technical
destinations resolve from existing catalogue records. The existing original rig
illustration and shared native details/summary presentation are reused.

Primary intent: drilling equipment procurement. Supporting questions: drilling
equipment RFQ checklist, well construction package boundaries, drilling spares
traceability. Current public primary-source results were checked; Search Console
was not accessed and no keyword volumes or ranking guarantees are claimed.
This complements the exploration and offshore guides and consumes no daily
blog-article or Google-post quota.

  governing standards and regulatory requirements:
  https://jip33.iogp.org/about/faq/

These public pages identify document scope and procurement context. Full
normative texts and project-specific well requirements were not available.

instructions or compliance promises are inferred. Package comparisons and RFQ
questions are original purchasing analysis. Manufacturer search pages were
sparse and were not used for model-specific claims; no supplier identities appear
