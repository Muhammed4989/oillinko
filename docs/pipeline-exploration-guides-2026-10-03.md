# Pipeline and exploration sector guides — 3 October 2026

The existing `/industries/pipelines` and `/industries/exploration` pages had catalogue filters and a short sector introduction, but no independent procurement guide. This addition uses the existing expandable IndustryGuide component, images and topic registry. No category, route, article or supplier assignment is created.

## Intent and boundaries

- Pipelines: coordinate purchasing boundaries between line pipe, stations, inspection and terminal measurement. This sector overview does not duplicate the equipment guides or give a pigging, isolation or testing procedure. Related existing topics provide the narrower requirements.
- Exploration: connect acquisition equipment, field services, sample identity and data handover. This differs from the reservoir software guide's license/model scope by focusing on field records and acceptance between disciplines.
- Both enquiries remain routed through Oillinko. No inventory, manufacturer relationship, price, operating limit or qualification is claimed. Manufacturing origin remains separate from delivery and service location.

## Primary sources reviewed on 3 October 2026

1. [PHMSA In-Line Inspections fact sheet](https://primis.phmsa.dot.gov/stakeholder-comms/factsheets/FSSmartPig/) — technical overview revised in 2014. Supports the limited explanation that inspection technologies have different capabilities and require pipe/threat information. Used as technical context, not evidence of current regulatory requirements or a universal detection guarantee. No deployment instructions or numerical limits are reproduced.
2. [SLB Geosolutions services](https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/innovating-in-oil-and-gas/reservoir-characterization/seismic/geosolutions-services) — identifies acquisition, processing, imaging and interpretation as related specialist workflows. Supports the distinction of procurement boundaries; no branded product description or performance claim is copied into the public guide.
3. [SEG technical standards](https://seg.org/publications/seg-technical-standards/) and [SEG-Y revision 2.1](https://seg.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/seg_y_rev2_1-oct2023.pdf) — support the need to identify file revision, headers, coordinate reference and units. The public guide does not mandate a specific revision, reproduce byte tables or imply that a file-format declaration proves compatibility.

Most text consists of original purchasing questions, proposed document boundaries and acceptance considerations, rather than a source paraphrase. Supplier names and source links remain in this internal editorial note only, in accordance with AGENTS.md. Existing generic illustrative images retain their meaningful alt text and native hover title through SiteImage.
