# Power and refinery procurement guide review

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Two original guides extend existing sector pages without adding categories or blog articles. The power guide separates electrical, steam, cooling and compressed-air package boundaries. The refinery guide distinguishes tagged replacement, revamp and turnaround enquiries. All related destinations resolve through the existing central catalogue IDs.

Primary research:

- [US Energy Information Administration: the refining process](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/refining-crude-oil-the-refining-process.php) supports the limited background distinction between separation, conversion and treatment. No refinery process settings, capacity figures or operating methods are reproduced.
- [US Department of Energy: steam systems](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ito/steam-systems) and its [steam system sourcebook](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2014/05/f15/steamsourcebook.pdf) support considering generation, distribution, end use and condensate within a system. No universal savings, operating settings or design calculations are claimed.

Most copy is original purchasing, interface and evidence planning. It requests the assigned project's design basis rather than selecting equipment or prescribing site work. Source identities are retained here for editorial review and are not presented as suppliers in the public guides. Existing licensed sector images use their meaningful English descriptions through SiteImage. A catalogue entry is not a stock, capability, appointment or acceptance promise; enquiries remain directed to Oillinko for manual review.
