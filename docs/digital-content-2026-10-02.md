## Primary research basis

- [ISO/IEC 19770-3:2016 public abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/52293.html): usage entitlements and their records are separate from activation mechanisms; original license documentation governs the actual terms. Only the public scope was consulted, not paid standard text. The guide asks for the offered rights rather than interpreting a particular contract.
- [NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final): operational technology has performance, reliability and safety constraints alongside security. The current page also identifies Revision 4 as an initial public draft; no draft requirement is prescribed as final or mandatory.
- [OPC UA Part 1 overview](https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-10000-1/4): communication and information models address different aspects of interoperability; site design determines required security arrangements. The copy asks for semantic mappings, ownership and configuration evidence. It does not prescribe a protocol, configuration or universal security claim.

The remaining text is original procurement and acceptance planning. It does not contain operating instructions or credentials. Illustration assets and existing internal links remain in place; site enquiries continue to Oillinko for manual review.
