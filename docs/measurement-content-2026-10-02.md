# Instrument purchasing copy review

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Four existing instrument pages now have individual specification copy and three original comparison/handover sections each. The copy requests actual duty data, interfaces and evidence without selecting a product, prescribing settings or claiming a system's acceptance.

Primary research:

- [WIKA pressure-reference explanation](https://www.wika.com/en-us/lp_differential_pressure.WIKA): gauge, absolute and differential measurements use different references. Only this basic distinction supports the copy; no numerical product limits or weather-error claims are reproduced.
- [WIKA thermowell overview](https://www.wika.com/en-en/thermowells_protection_tubes.WIKA): sensor fit, material, construction and process connection need assessment together. No insertion-length recommendation, live replacement instruction or design calculation is prescribed.
- [Endress+Hauser flow-measurement overview](https://www.endress.com/en/field-instruments-overview/flow-measurement-product-overview): measuring principle, fluid and measurement quantity distinguish offered arrangements. The copy requests configuration-specific evidence rather than universal suitability.
- [Emerson level-measurement overview](https://www.emerson.com/en/measurement-instrumentation/catalog/level-measurement): different level arrangements and applications require different assessment inputs. The copy does not repeat model-specific ranges, accuracy, approvals or product-selection claims.

These official manufacturer sources are an internal editorial basis, not appointed suppliers. Their identities and links do not appear in the public instrument pages. Other paragraphs are original enquiry and evidence planning. Existing central topic IDs, canonical URLs, old information-N anchors and supplier-neutral Oillinko enquiry routing remain unchanged. No new category, blog article or Google post is created.
