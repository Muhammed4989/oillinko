# Workshop, protection, flexible connections and logistics copy

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Four existing catalogue topics now have individual specification paragraphs and three original purchasing sections each. The copy replaces generic requirement text, distinguishes scope boundaries and requests evidence for the actual offered configuration. It gives no operating, application, pressure-test or live-work instructions.

Primary editorial checks:

- [ICC Incoterms 2020 overview](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/incoterms-2020/): delivery rules allocate aspects of transport, costs and risk. The copy asks for the agreed rule, edition and named place; it does not choose a rule for a transaction or interpret a particular contract.
- [ISO 12944-2:2017 public abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/64834.html): environmental exposure is relevant to protective paint system selection. Atmospheric, immersed and buried duties are distinguished without assigning a category, lifetime, thickness or universal product requirement. Only the public scope was reviewed.
- [IEC 60974-1:2021 official scope](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/64782): welding power-source safety and performance requirements have a defined equipment scope. No equipment standard is presented as approval of a welding procedure or a complete fabrication package.
- [ISO/TS 17165-2:2018 public abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/74155.html): hydraulic hose guidance has a defined assembly scope. It is not applied as a universal process-hose or expansion-joint specification. Only the public scope was reviewed; no paid technical text or installation procedure was reproduced.

All other paragraphs are original enquiry, comparison and handover planning. Supply availability, country of manufacture, technical conformity and service assignments remain subject to the actual offer. Supplier names, websites and imagery are excluded from these public catalogue pages. Existing central IDs, canonical routes and information-N fragment targets are preserved. This is supplementary catalogue editing, not additional blog publication.
