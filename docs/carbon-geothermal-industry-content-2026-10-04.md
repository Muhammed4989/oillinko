# Carbon management and geothermal sector guide

The existing `/industries/energy-transition` page now has an independent purchasing guide covering capture inlet data, CO2 conditioning boundaries, methane measurement objectives, geothermal resource inputs, acceptance records and commercial comparisons. Equipment supply is distinguished from project performance, carbon-credit eligibility and specialist execution. Enquiries continue to Oillinko for manual coordination.

The guide uses the central industry and catalogue registries, existing server-rendered native expander and original geothermal illustration. It adds no route, taxonomy, supplier identity or environmental performance claim. Six descriptive links connect the four sector equipment families with emissions surveys and material inspection.

Research reviewed 4 October 2026:

- [Point Source Carbon Capture](https://netl.doe.gov/carbon-management/carbon-capture): indexed primary-source text identifies different capture processes; direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. No numerical performance claim is derived from it.
- [Enhanced Geothermal Systems](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/geothermal/enhanced-geothermal-systems): hydrothermal, enhanced and closed-loop arrangements differ; equipment needs project-specific resource and interface review. No reservoir development procedure is provided.
- [Global Methane Tracker 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2026): measurement campaigns and satellite observations inform methane assessment; the guide separates purchaser objectives and deliverables without copying emissions estimates.
- [JIP33 procurement specifications FAQ](https://jip33.iogp.org/about/faq/): technical, quality, information and project-specific inputs belong in the adopted procurement package.

Validation and deployment evidence are saved in the private publishing record. IndexNow receipt is a discovery notification, not evidence of indexing or search position. This supplementary guide does not consume the daily new-blog or Google-post quota.
